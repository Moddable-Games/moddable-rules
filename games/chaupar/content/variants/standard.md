## Chaupar (Standard)

The standard four-player form of Chaupar, played on a cross-shaped cloth board with three four-sided long dice. The game was played in the court of Mughal Emperor Akbar and is the game of dice described in the Mahabharata.

{{svg:chaupar-board.svg "Chaupar — cross-shaped board layout"}}

### Equipment

| Item | Quantity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| **Board** | 1 | Cross-shaped cloth board; three columns of eight squares per arm; Charkoni in centre |
| **Pieces** | 16 | 4 per player; two teams: Black&nbsp;&amp;&nbsp;Yellow vs Red&nbsp;&amp;&nbsp;Green |
| **Long dice** | 3 | Four-sided oblong; faces show 1, 2, 5, 6 (or 1, 3, 5, 6 in some sets) |

### Setup

**Starting positions:** Pieces start on specific squares, not in the Charkoni. Count from the Charkoni exit along each player’s middle column: place two pieces on squares 6 and 7 of the middle column. The other two pieces are placed on squares 23 and 24 from the same counting origin, which fall in the outer row of the adjacent arm.

### Throwing

Throw all three long dice together. The total is the move value. There are no grace rolls. The throw may be split across multiple pieces in any way the player chooses.

**Example:** A roll of 1, 2, 6 (total 9) may move one piece 9 squares, or two pieces by 3 and 6, or three pieces by 1, 2, and 6.

### Movement

Pieces travel counter-clockwise around the board: down the middle column of the player’s own arm, around the outer columns of the other three arms, and back up the home arm’s middle column to the Charkoni.

**No castle squares:** Every square is capturable. There are no safe squares.

### Super-Pieces

Two or more same-colour pieces on the same square combine into a conglomerate piece and move as one. Capture immunity applies by size:

| Piece type | Vulnerable to |
|------------|---------------|
| Single | Any size |
| Double | Double, triple, or quadruple |
| Triple | Triple or quadruple |
| Quadruple | Quadruple only |

### Capture

Landing on a square occupied by a capturable opponent group removes that group to the Charkoni. Pieces return to the Charkoni and re-enter the board from their starting path.

**Tohd (must capture before finishing):** A player may not begin returning pieces to the Charkoni to finish until they have captured at least one opponent piece.

### Team Home Order

All Black pieces must finish before any Yellow piece may finish. All Red pieces must finish before any Green piece may finish.

### Winning

The first team to return all eight of their combined pieces to the Charkoni wins.
