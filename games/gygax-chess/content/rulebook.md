## Gygax Chess

A three-dimensional chess variant by Gary Gygax, published in *Dragon Magazine* No. 100 (TSR Inc., August 1985). Two players command 42 pieces each across three stacked 12×8 boards representing Air, Land, and Subterranean realms.
