## Hex Chess

A family of chess variants played on hexagonal boards. Each hex connects to six neighbours rather than eight, and pieces move along three axes rather than two. Piece types and the checkmate objective remain the same as standard chess; the geometry reshapes strategy.

The family spans boards from 37 to 91 hexes across six defined variants, each with its own pawn rules, board size, and orientation.

### Variants

<div class="variant-grid">

- [Gliński](variants/glinski/) — 91 hexes, the original and most widely played. 9 pawns per side, orthogonal pawn captures, 3/4&#8211;1/4 stalemate rule.
- [McCooey](variants/mccooey/) — 91 hexes, diagonal pawn captures, 7 pawns per side, stalemate is a draw.
- [Shafran](variants/shafran/) — 70 hexes, 9 files, castling permitted, variable pawn initial step by file.
- [De Vasa](variants/de-vasa/) — 81 hexes, horizontal rhombus orientation, pawns start on rank 3.
- [Brusky](variants/brusky/) — 84 hexes, horizontal, 10 pawns per side, expanded unmoved-pawn capture rule.
- [Mini Hexchess](variants/mini-hexchess/) — 37 hexes, no Queen piece, all opponent-side hexes are promotion squares. Invented Dave McCooey, 1997.
