## Quang Trung Chess

Quang Trung Chess (Cờ Quang Trung) is a Vietnamese chess variant associated with the Tây Sơn dynasty and its leader Nguyễn Huệ — who ruled as the Quang Trung Emperor from 1788 to 1792. It is played on a standard 9×10 board but uses a distinct piece set with Vietnamese names and significantly different movement rules from Xiangqi.

### The Board

Standard 9×10 Xiangqi board. Pieces occupy intersections. The river applies.

### Starting Position

Differs from standard Xiangqi. Pieces are arranged according to the Vietnamese military theme.

> **Verification note:** Confirm the exact starting position from chessvariants.com before building. The layout is distinct from standard Xiangqi and must be transcribed from primary sources.

### Piece Set

Quang Trung Chess uses Vietnamese military piece names with substantially different movements:

| Vietnamese Name | Approx. Role | Movement |
|---|---|---|
| Tướng (General) | King | One step orthogonally or diagonally within the Palace |
| Sĩ (Official) | Advisor | Varies — may move more freely than Xiangqi Advisor |
| Tượng (Đại) (Elephant) | Elephant-like | Movement may differ from Xiangqi |
| Mã (Horse) | Horse | May have longer range or different restriction |
| Xe (Chariot) | Rook-like | Slides orthogonally |
| Pháo (Cannon) | Cannon-like | Slide + platform capture |
| Tốt (Soldier) | Pawn | Advances; movement may differ post-river |

> **Verification note:** Exact piece movements must be confirmed from chessvariants.com. Vietnamese chess variants have been under-documented in English sources; the piece movements listed above are approximations and require primary-source verification.

### Win Condition

Checkmate or stalemate the opponent's General.
