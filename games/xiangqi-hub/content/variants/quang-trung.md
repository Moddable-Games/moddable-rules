## Quang Trung Chess

Quang Trung Chess (Cờ Quang Trung) is a Vietnamese chess variant associated with the Tây Sơn dynasty and its leader Nguyễn Huệ, who ruled as the Quang Trung Emperor from 1788 to 1792. It is played on a standard 9×10 board but uses a distinct piece set with Vietnamese names and different movement rules from Xiangqi.

- Win condition: checkmate or stalemate the opponent's General
- The piece set and movement rules differ substantially from standard Xiangqi
