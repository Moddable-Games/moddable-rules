## 4-Player Cribbage (Partnership)

4-Player Cribbage is a partnership game for two teams of two. Partners sit across from each other and combine their scores throughout the game.

### The Deal

Each player is dealt **5 cards** and discards **1 card** to the crib, giving each player a 4-card hand and a full 4-card crib.

### Teams

Players sit in alternating team order around the table: Team A — Team B — Team A — Team B. Partners' pegging scores and show scores are combined into a single team total on a shared cribbage board track.

### Pegging and Show

Identical to standard Cribbage. Pegging proceeds clockwise from the dealer's left. During the show, hands are scored clockwise from the dealer's left; each partner's points are added to their team's running total. The dealer scores their hand last, then scores the crib.

### Game

First team to reach **121 points** wins. Either partner reaching 121 on the shared track wins the game for the team.
