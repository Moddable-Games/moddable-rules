## CHARACTER SHEET

A blank character sheet for recording your Knave character's ability defences, inventory slots, and traits. Print this sheet or use the [interactive character generator](https://engine.moddable.games/play/?game=knave&variant=character-sheet) to create a new character with randomised abilities and starting equipment.
