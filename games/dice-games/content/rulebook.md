## Dice Games

A hub for games whose primary component is standard six-sided dice. Three games, three different relationships with probability: Yahtzee optimises a scorecard over 13 fixed categories; Liar’s Dice is a bluffing game of hidden information; Farkle is a press-your-luck game of accumulating and banking points.

### Standard Components

- **Dice:** standard six-sided dice (d6), pips 1–6
- **Cup:** for shaking and concealing dice (Liar’s Dice)
- **Scorecard:** required for Yahtzee only

<div class="variant-grid">

- [Yahtzee](variants/yahtzee/) — Roll 5 dice up to 3 times per turn. Fill 13 scoring categories over 13 rounds. Highest total wins.
- [Liar's Dice](variants/liars-dice/) — Hidden dice under cups. Bid on what faces are showing across all players. Challenge or be challenged.
- [Farkle](variants/farkle/) — Roll 6 dice, set aside scoring combinations, press your luck. Bust and lose it all, or bank and stay safe.
