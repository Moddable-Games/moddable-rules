## CHARACTER SHEET

A blank character sheet for recording your Cairn character's ability scores, HP, inventory slots, and background. Print this sheet or use the [interactive character generator](https://engine.moddable.games/play/?game=cairn&variant=character-sheet) to create a new character with randomised traits, gear, and spellbooks.
