## Cairn

Cairn is a classless, rules-light adventure game about exploring a dark and mysterious Wood filled with strange folk, hidden treasure, and unspeakable monstrosities. Character generation is quick and random, adventures are tense and reward careful exploration, and combat is frantic and deadly. Cairn was written by Yochai Gal, drawing on the Into the Odd lineage of OSR design.

A character's role or skills are not limited by a class. Instead, the equipment they carry and their experiences define their specialty. Death is always around the corner, but it is never random or without warning: the game favours fiction-first resolution over dice for difficulty, and reserves rolls for genuinely risky moments.
