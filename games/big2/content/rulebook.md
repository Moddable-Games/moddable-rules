## Overview

Big 2 (also known as Deuces, Pusoy Dos, or Chinese Poker) is a climbing card game for four players using a standard 52-card deck. Players take turns playing progressively higher combinations until all others pass. The goal is to be the first to empty your hand. The 2 of Spades is the highest card.

Widely popular across East and Southeast Asia, Big 2 has spawned numerous regional variants with different suit rankings, combination rules, and scoring systems.
