## Wildebeest Chess

A chess variant played on an expanded **11×10 board** that introduces two new fairy chess pieces: the **Camel** and the **Wildebeest**. The Camel is a (3,1) leaper (the camel's version of a knight's (2,1) leap). The Wildebeest combines the Knight and the Camel into a single powerful compound piece.

Designed by R. Wayne Schmittberger and published in *New Rules for Classic Games* (1992). Widely implemented in open-source chess engines.

### New Pieces

**Camel (C):** A (3,1) leaper — jumps to any square that is 3 steps in one direction and 1 step perpendicular, regardless of intervening pieces. Colour-bound: always lands on the same colour square.

**Wildebeest (W):** A compound of Knight + Camel. Can move to any square reachable by either a standard (2,1) knight leap or a (3,1) camel leap. Not colour-bound.
