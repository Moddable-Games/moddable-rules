## Dover Patrol

Published by H.P. Gibson & Sons, Britain, before 1932 (predating Tri-Tactics, which incorporated it). Dover Patrol transposes the L'Attaque system to a naval warfare setting on a section of the English Channel. Piece names reference Royal Navy vessel types; all rules are mechanically identical to Standard L'Attaque.

### Board

9 columns × 10 rows, matching Standard L'Attaque. Three 2-square impassable areas in the centre of the board represent minefields (corresponding to L'Attaque's lake areas).

### Piece Set (36 per player)

All movement and capture rules are identical to Standard L'Attaque. Piece correspondence:

| Dover Patrol Piece | L'Attaque Equivalent | Rank | Special Ability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flagship | Flag | — | Immovable; capturing it wins |
| Mine | Mine | — | Immovable; defeats all attackers except Mine Sweepers |
| Battleship | Commander-in-chief | 10 | Defeated only by Mine or attacking Submarine |
| Admiral | Brigadier General | 9 | Standard capture |
| Battle Cruiser | Colonel | 8 | Standard capture |
| Cruiser | Commandant | 7 | Standard capture |
| Torpedo Boat | Captain | 6 | Standard capture |
| Gunboat | Lieutenant | 5 | Standard capture |
| Patrol Boat | Sergeant | 4 | Standard capture |
| Mine Sweeper | Sapper | 3 | Only ship that can clear Mines |
| Destroyer | Scout | 2 | Slides unlimited orthogonally |
| Submarine | Spy | 1 | Defeats Battleship on attack; loses if Battleship attacks first |

### Rules

All rules (setup, movement, combat, hidden information, win conditions) are identical to Standard L'Attaque. Refer to the Standard L'Attaque rules for full details. The only differences are piece names and the naval theme.
