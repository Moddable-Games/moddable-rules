## Dover Patrol

Published by H.P. Gibson & Sons, Britain, circa 1930. Dover Patrol transposes the L'Attaque system to a naval warfare setting representing the English Channel. Piece names reference Royal Navy vessel types. All movement and capture mechanics are identical to Standard L'Attaque.

### Board

Same 9-column × 10-row grid as Standard L'Attaque. Obstacle squares (representing minefields or sea hazards) are in the same central positions. Players set up on their own half in rows 1–4 and rows 7–10.

### Piece Set

36 pieces per player. All movement and capture rules are identical to Standard L'Attaque. Naval piece names correspond to L'Attaque equivalents:

| Dover Patrol Piece | L'Attaque Equivalent | Rank | Special Ability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flagship | Flag | — | Immovable; capturing it wins |
| Mine | Mine | — | Immovable; defeats all attackers except Mine Sweepers |
| Submarine | Spy | 1 | Defeats Battleship on attack; loses if Battleship attacks it |
| Destroyer | Scout | 2 | Slides unlimited orthogonally |
| Mine Sweeper | Sapper | 3 | Only piece that can clear Mines |
| Patrol Boat | Sergeant | 4 | Standard capture |
| Gunboat | Lieutenant | 5 | Standard capture |
| Torpedo Boat | Captain | 6 | Standard capture |
| Cruiser | Commandant | 7 | Standard capture |
| Battle Cruiser | Colonel | 8 | Standard capture |
| Admiral | Brigadier General | 9 | Standard capture |
| Battleship | Commander-in-chief | 10 | Highest rank; defeated by Submarine when Submarine attacks |

> **Note on piece counts:** Dover Patrol uses the same 36-piece count as Standard L'Attaque (1 Flagship + 4 Mines + 1 Submarine + 8 Destroyers + 4 Mine Sweepers + 4 Patrol Boats + 4 Gunboats + 4 Torpedo Boats + 2 Cruisers + 2 Battle Cruisers + 1 Admiral + 1 Battleship = 36). Confirm exact counts from an original H.P. Gibson edition if available.

### Rules

All rules (setup, movement, attack, hidden information, win conditions) are identical to Standard L'Attaque. The only differences are piece names and the naval theme. Refer to the Standard L'Attaque rules for full details.
