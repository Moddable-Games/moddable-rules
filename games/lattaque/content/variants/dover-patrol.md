## Dover Patrol

Published by H.P. Gibson & Sons, Britain, circa 1930. Dover Patrol transposes the L’Attaque system to a naval warfare setting. Piece names reference Royal Navy vessels; the board represents a section of the English Channel. Rules are mechanically identical to L’Attaque with naval-themed piece names.

### Board

10 × 9 board. Minefield squares (corresponding to L’Attaque’s lakes) are impassable, located in the same central positions. Sea lane squares (along the outer edges) have no special movement rules in the standard game.

### Piece Set

All movement and capture rules are identical to Standard L’Attaque. Piece correspondence:

| Dover Patrol Piece | L’Attaque Equivalent | Rank | Special Ability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flagship | Flag | 0 | Immovable; capturing it wins |
| Mine | Bomb | B | Immovable; defeats all attackers except Mine Sweepers |
| Submarine | Spy | 1 | Defeats Battleship on attack; loses if Battleship attacks it |
| Destroyer | Scout | 2 | Slides unlimited orthogonally |
| Mine Sweeper | Miner | 3 | Only piece that can clear Mines |
| Patrol Boat | Sergeant | 4 | Standard capture |
| Gunboat | Lieutenant | 5 | Standard capture |
| Torpedo Boat | Captain | 6 | Standard capture |
| Cruiser | Major | 7 | Standard capture |
| Battle Cruiser | Colonel | 8 | Standard capture |
| Admiral | General | 9 | Standard capture |
| Battleship | Marshal | 10 | Highest rank; defeated by Submarine when Submarine attacks |

### Rules

All rules (setup, movement, attack, hidden information, win conditions) are identical to Standard L’Attaque. Refer to the Standard L’Attaque rules for full details. The only differences are piece names and the naval theme.
