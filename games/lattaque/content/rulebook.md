# L'Attaque

L'Attaque (also written L'Attaque) is a hidden-information strategy game designed by Hermance Edan, patented in France in 1908 and marketed in Britain by H.P. Gibson & Sons. It is the direct ancestor of Stratego and the founding game of the hidden-rank strategy game family.

Both players lay out an army of pieces face-down. The ranks of pieces are secret until two pieces attempt to occupy the same square, at which point the lower rank is removed. The object is to capture the opponent’s Flag.
