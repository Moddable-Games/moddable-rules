## Alice Chess

A chess variant invented by V. R. Parton in 1953, named after Lewis Carroll's *Through the Looking-Glass*. Two boards are in play simultaneously. After every move, the piece that moved transfers through the looking-glass to the corresponding square on the other board. A piece may only move if the destination on the other board is unoccupied.
