## Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition SRD

The Systems Reference Document 5.1 (SRD 5.1) contains the open core rules for D&D 5th Edition, released by Wizards of the Coast under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). This hub provides the full SRD in browsable, searchable form alongside machine-readable data files.
