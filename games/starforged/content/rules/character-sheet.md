## CHARACTER SHEET

A blank character sheet for recording your Starforged character's stats, legacies, vows, and assets. Print this sheet or use the [interactive character generator](https://engine.moddable.games/play/?game=starforged&variant=character-sheet) to create a new character with randomised starting assets and sector truths.
