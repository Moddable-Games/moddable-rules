## Dungeon Chess (Four Player (20×20))

{{svg:four-player-board.svg "Dungeon Chess (Four Player (20×20)) — starting position"}}

The four-player Dungeon Chess map. A large 20×20 arena with deploy zones in each corner, supporting free-for-all or team (2v2) play.

### Map Features

- **Corner deployment:** Each player occupies a corner zone
- **Central arena:** Open ground in the middle creates a contested no-man's-land
- **Large scale:** 400 squares give room for complex alliances and betrayals

### Differences from Standard

Four-player games introduce diplomacy and timing. Attacking early exposes your flank to the other two players. The winner is typically the player who engages last while their opponents weaken each other. Team play (2v2) removes the diplomacy element and plays more like two parallel two-player games that can interfere with each other.
